## Fast read

The contest is not a generic “build any AI app” competition. A competitive entry needs a **working, India-scale, public-sector workflow** with Google AI performing a consequential task; realistic data; a live link; a 3–5 minute demo; and a 10–12 slide deck. The highest-weighted criterion is technical execution (25%), and the final 20% is whether a ministry/state could pilot it quickly.

For a solo entrant, keep the geography bounded to a pilot district/corridor while explicitly showing a state/national data contract and scale-out architecture. Do not try to build a national platform literally.
