In Fani, 45 of the 57 substations that went dark had been flagged at 50 percent or more; that is in-sample, which is why the previous slide matters. With the as-issued ECMWF ensemble, per-site odds exist 68 hours out. And the money: districts where the illustrative parametric cover triggered lost power at 18 percent of their lit substations, against zero where it did not, so the trigger tracks real losses.
