Gemini never sets a probability (the trained outage model does), never sees the outcome during a forecast replay, and issues nothing without the officer's signed approval. Every decision goes to an append-only Firestore audit log, and IMD remains the authority.

Gemini on Vertex AI does six jobs here. Gemini 3.8 Flash reads IMD's official bulletin straight from the PDF into a checked structure, so the Brief shows the official forecast next to ours. It looks at the satellite night lights before and after the storm and says where the lights went out; for Fani it names Khordha, Puri and Cuttack, which agrees with our model. The officer can simply call it: a live voice call over Gemini Live, in Hindi, Odia or any Indian language, where the analyst answers aloud with numbers from our geo service and can be interrupted like a colleague. Typed chat replies come in six languages and can be read aloud. It explains ranks using live tool calls, drafts the CAP alert in three languages, and once the officer approves, the alert is published on the CAP feed and spoken aloud. Gemini never sets a number, never sees the outcome in a forecast, and issues nothing without a signed human approval.
