Gemini never sets a probability (the model's code does), never sees the outcome during a forecast replay, and issues nothing without the officer's signed approval. Every decision goes to an append-only Firestore audit log, and IMD remains the authority.

Gemini 3.8 Flash on Vertex AI does six jobs, all multimodal and all live. It reads IMD's official bulletin straight from the PDF into a checked structure, so the Brief shows the official forecast next to ours. It looks at the satellite night lights before and after the storm and says where the lights went out; for Fani it names Khordha, Puri and Cuttack, which agrees with our model. It hears the officer's spoken question directly, in Odia or Hindi. It explains ranks using live tool calls, drafts the CAP alert in three languages, and once the officer approves, the alert is published on the CAP feed and spoken aloud. What it never does: set a number, see the outcome in a forecast, or issue anything without a signed human approval.
