ShadowCast

Names the hospitals, shelters and substations a cyclone will knock out, then checks itself against satellite data.

PILOT: CYCLONE FANI × ODISHA COAST
TEAM ARGMAX · SOLO BUILD

shadowcast-two.vercel.app · github.com/tsathya98/shadowcast

ShadowCast is impact-based cyclone forecasting for the officer who has to act. Instead of a track and a cone, it names the hospitals, shelters and substations that will lose power, says when the wind reaches each one, drafts the local-language alert with Gemini, and afterwards checks itself against satellite night lights. Built solo by Team Argmax, live on Vercel and Google Cloud.
