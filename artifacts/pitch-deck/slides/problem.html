02

Fani is remembered as an evacuation success, with 1.2 million people moved in time. But eleven days later Puri district still had no mains power, and hospitals and water works depend on it. The officer we build for has about 48 hours and a list of more than 3,000 sites. Today nothing tells them which of these sites will go dark, or when.
