SHADOWCAST · TEAM ARGMAX

07

Every layer is real and public. Tracks come from IBTrACS, forecasts are ECMWF's ensemble exactly as issued. The official side is IMD's bulletins, which Gemini reads, plus NDMA's SACHET warnings and GDACS for the live feed. Sites and arterial roads come from OpenStreetMap and Odisha's shelter list. Earth Engine supplies population, district boundaries, bare-earth coastal ground, sea-floor depth for the surge model, and the two truths we score against: NASA's night lights for outages and NASA's GPM satellite for rain.
