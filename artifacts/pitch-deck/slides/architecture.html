Everything on this slide is running now. The console and Gemini routes are on Vercel. Gemini runs on Vertex AI with tools and PDF, image and audio input, and every officer decision and every Gemini reading lands in Firestore. The Python geo service on Cloud Run computes wind, surge, rain, road closures and parametric triggers from scenarios built with Earth Engine, and serves the live feed a scheduled Cloud Run job archives every six hours. An approved alert is published on a standard CAP feed. There are no stored keys and no local storage: everything is in Cloud Storage and Firestore's free tier. Test coverage and CI are in place because a state agency will ask for them. Running cost: for one Odisha cyclone season with 30 district officers we estimate about 5,300 rupees a month at today's Gemini prices, rising to about 8,800 when introductory pricing ends. Cloud Run, Firestore, Scheduler, Maps and Earth Engine all stay inside their free allowances; Gemini is the main cost, plus Vercel's paid plan.
