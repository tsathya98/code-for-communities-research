Everything on this slide is running now, and data flows left to right. Public data comes in on the left: Earth Engine for night lights, GPM rain and terrain; IBTrACS tracks, ECMWF forecasts as issued and OpenStreetMap sites; and the official feeds from IMD, GDACS and NDMA SACHET. The offline build turns it into scenarios in Cloud Storage, and a Cloud Scheduler job archives the live feeds every six hours. The geo service on Cloud Run computes wind, surge, rain and road closures, runs the outage model we trained on satellite-seen outages, and bridges live voice calls to Gemini Live. On Vertex AI, Gemini 3.8 Flash is the duty analyst with tools, reading PDFs, images and voice notes and drafting CAP alerts; Gemini Live handles real-time calls; Gemini TTS speaks alerts; Firestore holds the audit log. The officer uses the console on Vercel, which signs in to Google Cloud through Workload Identity Federation, so no keys are stored, and approved alerts go out on a standard CAP feed. Running cost for one Odisha season with 30 officers is about 5,300 rupees a month at today's Gemini prices, about 8,800 when introductory pricing ends; live calls add roughly 3 to 5 US cents a minute.
