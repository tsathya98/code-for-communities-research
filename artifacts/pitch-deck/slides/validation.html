Three models, each scored against independent truth, and on the right the live Prove tab, where Gemini reads the same before and after night-light images and agrees that Khordha, Puri and Cuttack went dark. Outages: the outage model is a logistic regression we trained on Fani, on 200 lit substations whose outages the satellite saw. It scores 0.97, and 0.97 again when each stretch of coast is hidden in turn; on Hudhud, a storm it never saw, 0.79. Surge: the physics model lands in the right range for all four storms against IMD's reports, running high on narrow shelves, which is typical of this kind of model. Rain: rank correlation with NASA's GPM satellite is 0.72 to 0.96, except Amphan. Amphan fails on both outages and rain, and we publish that. A physics fix we tried made Fani worse, so we rejected it by a rule set in advance.
